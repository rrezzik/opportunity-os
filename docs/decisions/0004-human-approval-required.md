# 0004: Human Approval for Consequential Actions

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-04

## Context

AI extraction and generation can be inaccurate, and job applications contain consequential personal representations.

## Decision

Require human approval before extracted records enter trusted aggregates and before applications, messages, scheduling, or tailored materials are used externally.

## Consequences

- AI output remains visibly provisional until approval.
- The system must retain correction and approval history.
- Autonomous external submission is outside the initial scope.
